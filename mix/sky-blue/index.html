How to mix sky blue

#8FBFE6

A light, clear blue. A real sky is lighter and slightly greener near the horizon, so mix two values.

Sky blue in acrylic

Buy the paints for this mix

No single acrylic paint comes close to sky blue, so mixing is the way to get it. You need these:

Titanium white PW6BlickJackson's ArtAmazon
French ultramarine PB29BlickJackson's ArtAmazon
Phthalo blue (green shade) PB15:3BlickJackson's ArtAmazon

Mix it from 10 basic paints

  • 30 parts Titanium white 95%
  • 1 part French ultramarine 3%
  • ½ part Phthalo blue (green shade) 2%

Near-exact match.

  1. Put out 30 parts of titanium white. A part is any equal measure: a pea-sized blob, a knife tip or a brushful.
  2. Add 1 part of French ultramarine, a little at a time, and mix it in completely before you judge the colour.
  3. Add ½ part of phthalo blue (green shade), a little at a time, and mix it in completely before you judge the colour.
  4. Acrylic dries a little darker than it looks wet, so let a test dab dry before you mix a large batch.
  5. Put a dab next to your reference and squint: if it looks too light, too dark or too bright, adjust in small steps.

Mix it from 10 basic paints

  • 2 parts French ultramarine 67%
  • 1 part Phthalo blue (green shade) 33%

Close match. Thinned to a pale wash with plenty of water.

  1. Put out 2 parts of French ultramarine. A part is any equal measure: a pea-sized blob, a knife tip or a brushful.
  2. Add 1 part of phthalo blue (green shade), a little at a time, and mix it in completely before you judge the colour.
  3. Thin the mix with water to a pale wash with plenty of water.
  4. Watercolour dries lighter than it looks wet. Mix a little stronger than you want and test on a scrap of the same paper.
  5. Put a dab next to your reference and squint: if it looks too light, too dark or too bright, adjust in small steps.

Tips for mixing blues

  • Blues are strong, phthalo blue especially. Add the blue to the lighter colour in very small amounts, never the other way round.
  • Ultramarine leans violet and phthalo blue leans green. Start from the blue that already leans the way you need.
